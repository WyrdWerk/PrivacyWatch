## AkashML — research note (2026-09-13)

Pages read: https://akashml.com/terms (ToS), https://akashml.com/privacy (privacy policy).

- TRAINING: off. PP: "We do not use Voice Samples, Voiceprints, user-uploaded images, prompts, generated Outputs, or other User Content to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model, whether our own or any third party's."
- RETENTION: "we do not retain prompts or transient inputs after the request has been processed"; exception for user-saved content (voice-library uploads). ToS: the content license terminates when the user deletes the content or closes the account; copies may be kept for limited periods per the privacy policy for compliance/disputes.
- ZDR: transient processing by design; retained only when the user affirmatively saves items. Voice features carry biometric consent rules (Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, Washington MHMDA) with defined deletion terms.
- LOCATION: decentralized: "the Services are hosted on the Akash Network, a decentralized compute network operated by third-party node operators."
- INCIDENTS: none found.
- Confidence: medium.
- Notes: analytics are de-identified/aggregated and "not used to train AI models".
