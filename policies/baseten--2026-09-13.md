# Baseten — API (Model Inference) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.baseten.co/terms-and-conditions

## Findings
- Training: Customer Agreement and DPA are silent on training Baseten-operated models on Customer Content; terms only define "Customer Model" as "Customer's machine learning model(s) that Customer will be deploying through the Baseten Products & Services".
- Retention: DPA: Customer may request return/deletion "no later than fourteen (14) days after the Cessation Date ('Post-cessation Storage Period')"; after expiry Baseten "shall promptly... either (at its option) delete; or render anonymous, all Customer Personal Data then within Baseten's possession".
- ZDR: No published no-training or zero-retention tier; the DPA covers personal-data return/deletion but does not address model training.
- Location: No data-center or processing-location statement in the Customer Agreement, DPA, or privacy policy; Delaware governing law, San Francisco Bay Area company.
- Incident: Confirmed incident on record (see Notes).
