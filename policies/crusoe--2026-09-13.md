# Crusoe — API (Managed Inference) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://legal.crusoe.ai/open-router#managed-inference-tos-open-router

## Findings
- Training: OpenRouter Managed Inference ToS: "Crusoe will only use Customer Data for inference - we will not use it for training Models, and we will not store it on disk or use it for any other purpose other than the inference process"; also "We do not use Inputs or Outputs to train Models or improve our Services without Customer's explicit opt-in consent."
- Retention: "Crusoe does not store Inputs and Outputs to disk, and only processes them as long as necessary to provide Customer with the Services"; in-memory request caches are wiped "when the model instance is deleted".
- ZDR: No-disk-storage + no-training are defaults of the OpenRouter Managed Inference service (ToS v4.6, effective July 23, 2026), not a paid tier.
- Location: Privacy Notice: "Crusoe and Crusoe's partners maintain data centers around the world, and provide the Services from these locations"; EEA/UK/Swiss transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and the International Data Transfer Agreement.
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
