# Deepgram — API (Speech) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://deepgram.com/terms

## Findings
- Training: "We may also use Your Content to improve our Services... including training and testing our Models." Opt-out per request: add mip_opt_out=true to API requests.
- Retention: Default retention for non-opted-out requests is not documented; "Data from opted-out requests is retained only for the duration necessary to process the request".
- ZDR: Self-serve exclusion from training and retention via mip_opt_out=true on each request; no tier restriction stated.
- Location: "We store data on servers in the U.S."

## Notes
Model Improvement Partnership Program documents the opt-out parameter. General privacy policy: customer-data retention governed by the business agreement.
