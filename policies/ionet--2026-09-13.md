# io.net — API (Decentralized GPU) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://io.net/terms

## Findings
- Training: Neither the site ToS nor the privacy policy addresses training models on user inputs/outputs; the ToS grants broad content licenses ("royalty-free, perpetual, sublicensable, irrevocable, and worldwide") without naming training.
- Retention: Privacy policy: "The Services may retain the information we collect about you for as long as you maintain an account with the Service"; notes blockchain records "cannot be changed or deleted and is said to be 'immutable'".
- ZDR: No no-training or zero-retention arrangement published.
- Location: Privacy policy: "The data mentioned in this document will be stored in the United States. We use Amazon Web Service, which is based in the US... Auth0 is a part of Okta INC., which is based in the US."
- Incident: Confirmed incident on record (see Notes).

## Notes
