# Liquid AI — API (Foundation Models) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.liquid.ai/terms-conditions

## Findings
- Training: "We may use outputs or inputs (collectively, 'Content') in perpetuity for our internal business purposes, including to develop new products and services, improve our Services, operate the Services, and maintain the Services"; the privacy policy names the purpose: "Training, fine-tuning or otherwise enhancing AI models and related technologies using inputs and outputs you share."
- Retention: Content may be used "in perpetuity for our internal business purposes"; the privacy policy sets no fixed retention period ("as long as necessary").
- ZDR: No no-training or zero-retention option appears in the ToS or privacy policy.
- Location: No processing-location statement; Massachusetts courts chosen for disputes; EU/UK privacy-rights sections present.
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
