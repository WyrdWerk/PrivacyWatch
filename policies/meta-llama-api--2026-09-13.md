# Meta — API (Llama / Model API) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://ai.developer.meta.com/legal/terms-of-service

## Findings
- Training: "[Meta] will not use Content from Standard Services to train Meta Models." Discounted/Contributor tiers permit Meta to "use your Content to train, develop, evaluate, and improve" its AI systems; no per-request exclusion on discounted traffic.
- Retention: Content/Usage Data retained "as needed" for service provision, law, enforcement, safety; no ordinary maximum stated. Account tools allow viewing/deleting Content.
- ZDR: ZDR for qualified organizations via separate enablement: prompts/responses not stored after return; flagged policy-violation content may be kept up to two years.
- Location: Terms and Data Commitments do not identify processing/storage regions.
