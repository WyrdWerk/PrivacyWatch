# Modal — API (Serverless GPU) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://modal.com/legal/terms

## Findings
- Training: SaaS Agreement §Use of AI Tools: "Modal will not, except with Customer's prior written consent: (i) train any AI model using Customer Data, or (ii) export Customer Data into, or cause Customer Data to be ingested by, large language models."
- Retention: "Customer Data processed by the Service is expunged promptly following completion of processing" (ToS/DPA, citing the security page); service accounts are deleted "within 60 days following the termination date".
- ZDR: No-training and prompt-expungement are platform defaults; customers can additionally pin processing to chosen regions. No separate ZDR SKU is documented.
- Location: "Customer may configure the Service to process Customer Data only on servers in the geographic regions Customer specifies... Modal will not process Customer Data on servers in other regions, except with Customer's prior written consent."
- Incident: Not flagged — the July 2026 rogue-agent event was a customer-account compromise on a public endpoint, not a breach of Modal's platform (kept below the confirmed-incident bar).
