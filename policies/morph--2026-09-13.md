## Morph — research note (2026-09-13)

Pages read: https://www.morphllm.com/privacy/tos (ToS, updated July 6 2025), https://www.morphllm.com/privacy (privacy policy).

- TRAINING: off by default with support/debug exception. ToS: "No training by default — your code is not used to train our models, except when you request support or we're debugging issues." Free tier §5.1: during support Morph may "train our models to prevent similar issues, and improve service quality"; paid tier adds confidentiality protections (§5.2).
- RETENTION: tiered — free: "up to 90 days" then deleted/anonymized; paid: "30-day data retention with secure deletion"; enterprise: "Code and content processed exclusively in memory and never written to persistent storage."
- ZDR: yes — enterprise tier: "Zero Training: We do not use your submissions to train or improve our models. Period."; immediate purge, no content logging, no human access without explicit grant.
- LOCATION: PP: "Our primary data processing occurs in the United States, with additional processing in select countries where our service providers operate"; SCCs for EEA/UK/Swiss transfers; self-hosted keeps data in your environment; Managed Instance is "hosted within your private cloud environment".
- INCIDENTS: none found.
- Confidence: medium-high.
- Notes: rated guarded because free/paid tiers carry the support/debugging training exception; enterprise tier removes it.
