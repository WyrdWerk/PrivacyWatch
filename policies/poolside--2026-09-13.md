# Poolside — API (Code Models) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.poolside.ai/legal/eula

## Findings
- Training: Poolside Terms of Use §4: "We may use your Content to provide, maintain, improve, and develop the Products and other Poolside offerings, including training our models, unless you opt-out. If you access the Products directly through the Site, you may opt-out of Poolside using your Content for training by selecting the Training Opt-Out under [settings]." Content "means any content, data, or information you submit to or receive through the Site, including prompts, code, output, and feedback."
- Retention: Privacy policy: "We retain Personal Data about you for as long as necessary to provide you with our Services"; the DPA requires return or deletion of Personal Data on termination at the customer's choice.
- ZDR: Self-serve Training Opt-Out in account settings for direct site access; no contractual ZDR tier published.
- Location: No processing-location statement in the Terms of Use or privacy policy; California governing law; DPA covers hosted and customer-virtual-cloud deployments.
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
