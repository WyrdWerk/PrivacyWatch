# Reka AI — API (Multimodal LLM) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://reka.ai/legal/terms-of-use

## Findings
- Training: "If you use the Services on a free basis, you acknowledge and agree that Reka may use Your Content to train, develop, and improve its machine learning models"; paid requests: "Reka will not use Your Content for model training unless you have explicitly opted in. You may provide or withdraw consent at any time through your account settings or by contacting Reka support."
- Retention: "Reka has no obligation to store any of Your Content"; files shared with third-party tools use "time-limited, private URLs... deleted after a short period (e.g., 24 hours)".
- ZDR: No-training is the default for paid API requests (opt-in only for training); free-tier use trains by default.
- Location: Privacy policy: "We are headquartered in the United States and may use service providers that operate in other countries. Your personal information may be transferred to the United States..."
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
