# Runway — API (Video Generation) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://runwayml.com/terms-of-use

## Findings
- Training: "You acknowledge that Inputs and Outputs may be used by the Company to train and improve its AI models, algorithms and related technology, products and services (including for labeling, classification, content moderation and model training purposes)" with a "non-exclusive, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid, transferable, sublicensable" license. No training opt-out appears in the ToS or privacy policy.
- Retention: "Company has no obligation to store any of Your Content"; privacy policy: information retained "only as long as necessary to provide the Service and for the other legitimate business purposes... we will retain face scans and voiceprints for no longer than the earlier of the date the initial purpose for collecting the data is satisfied or within three years of your last interaction with us."
- ZDR: No no-training or zero-retention tier is published.
- Location: "The Services are controlled and offered by Company from its facilities in the United States of America"; privacy policy confirms US headquarters with transfers to the US and other countries.
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
