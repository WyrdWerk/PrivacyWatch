# Sakana AI — API (Model Platform) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://console.sakana.ai/terms-of-service

## Findings
- Training: "The Company uses Content for machine learning model training, improvement, and other information analysis (including retraining and fine-tuning) ('Training Use')"; opt-out: "You may request that the Company stop using Your Content for Training Use... via the method set out on the screen through which the Company provides the Service."
- Retention: "The Company is not obligated to retain or store any text, files, instructions (prompts), or other data that You input... and Company may delete part or all of any Content, with or without prior notice"; it is also "not obligated to delete information like the weights of models trained based on Content, temporary caches of external vendors, or audit logs."
- ZDR: An in-product opt-out suspends Training Use prospectively; "the effects of those uses may not be reversible retroactively... even if You later opt out."
- Location: "The Service is provided for users in countries or regions other than the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland ('Supported Regions')"; privacy policy: data "may be transferred to third parties located outside your country of residence, including to Japan and the United States."
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
