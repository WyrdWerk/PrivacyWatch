# Sourceful — API (Image Generation) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.sourceful.com/legal/spring-terms-of-use

## Findings
- Training: "If you have a Paid Subscription, any and all User Materials uploaded by you and any and all Results generated by you will not be used by Sourceful: (i) to train generative AI components of Sourceful Platform; (ii) in our public index of images." No equivalent commitment is stated for free-tier use; users may not use outputs to train ML models.
- Retention: "We will only retain your personal information for as long as we need it unless we are required to keep it for longer to comply with our legal, accounting or regulatory requirements."
- ZDR: No-training applies to Paid Subscriptions only; free-tier training posture is undocumented.
- Location: Privacy policy: systems are "hosted by Google Cloud on their UK and/or European Servers"; England and Wales governing law (Sourceful Ltd, Manchester).
- Incident: None found (confirmed breach, regulatory action, or government ban) as of 2026-09-13.
