# StepFun — API (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://platform.stepfun.ai/docs/en/agreement/userservice

## Findings
- Training: Ordinary API terms grant a broad license to use prompts/outputs/logs "for any purpose"; training/fine-tuning is expressly identified for early-access and startup programs. Opt-out: avoid the program or contact StepFun for alternatives.
- Retention: Customer Data deleted within 30 days of termination/expiration, subject to legal, dispute, and abuse-prevention exceptions.
- ZDR: No ZDR or no-training tier promised; objecting customers are told to avoid the applicable program.
- Location: No processing country named; DPA refers generally to international transfers of Customer Personal Data.

## Notes
⚠ CISA/NSA/FBI joint advisory AA26-251A (2026-09-08) alleges high-volume unauthorized model-distillation campaigns — a government advisory, not a confirmed breach. Data-processing agreement: platform.stepfun.ai/docs/en/agreement/dataprocess.
