# Upstage — API (Solar) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.upstage.ai/terms-of-service

## Findings
- Training: Input/Output data not used for service improvement or AI training "unless it has obtained prior separate consent". Free-tier data is the exception: it "may be used... for research and development of artificial intelligence technologies (including training)".
- Retention: "The Company does not store Member Input/Output Data" except where operationally necessary; asynchronous requests retained until completion, results 30 days.
- ZDR: Paid/non-free API data is no-training and nominally not stored; no separately named ZDR program. Free tier excluded.
- Location: Conversations/uploads may be transferred to AWS in the United States for storage; no exclusive residency guarantee.

## Notes
AI input/output rules in Article 22 of the Terms. Revised terms effective 2026-09-21 carry materially the same training language.
