## Venice — research note (2026-09-13)

Pages read: https://venice.ai/legal/tos (ToS), https://venice.ai/legal/privacy-policy (privacy policy), venice.ai/privacy (short privacy page).

- TRAINING: off, contractually. ToS §10.2: "Venice.ai agrees that it shall not use, and shall require that third-party large language model providers ('Third-Party LLM Providers') do not use, any User Content to train any machine learning, deep learning, or statistical learning algorithms, LLMs, neural networks, or models."
- RETENTION: zero retention via providers. PP: "We operate a zero data retention policy with our model providers in which our model providers are prohibited from storing, retaining, or using any of your Prompts or Outputs beyond the time strictly necessary to process and return a response."
- ZDR: yes, by design. ZDR applies with model providers; likeness generation routes to BytePlus (Singapore), which "does not use your likeness data for any unrelated purpose" and Venice does not store likeness/biometric data on its servers.
- LOCATION: US-based service; inference via third-party open model providers (e.g., BytePlus in Singapore for likeness features).
- INCIDENTS: none found (searches returned no confirmed breach or regulatory action).
- Confidence: high.
- Notes: consumer design keeps chat history locally in the browser; ToS grants Venice a hosting license that terminates for deleted content. API+docs at docs.venice.ai use the same §10.2 terms.
