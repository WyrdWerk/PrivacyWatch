# Voyage AI — API (Embeddings) (research note, 2026-09-13)

Primary source: https://www.voyageai.com/tos

## Findings
- Training: "Unless you 'opt out'... you grant Voyage AI... [the right] to train, improve, and otherwise further develop the Service." Opt-out is an organization-admin dashboard toggle; requires a payment method on file. Applies only to subsequently submitted content.
- Retention: Default retention undocumented; opted-out content "immediately deleted by Voyage AI after it is processed for you" ("zero-day retention").
- ZDR: Zero-day retention for Voyage-hosted endpoints once a payment method is on file; free credits may be voided.
- Location: Non-US data may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

## Notes
DPA requires prompt deletion of Customer Personal Data at termination but specifies no default request auto-deletion period.
